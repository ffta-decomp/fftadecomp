# Fixed-O2 recovery throughput: measured results and selection

Updated 2026-10-22 after the first concentration pilot. Small methods had higher
success rates in the historical sample; 81–160 B methods delivered more exact
bytes per gross method hour. The prospective pilot did not establish a
concentration advantage. Repeated scalar source structure and established
field/helper contracts are better-supported selection clues than size alone.
No causal model advantage, fivefold gain or whole-game completion is established.
